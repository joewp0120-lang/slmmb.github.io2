Tech Highlight: Low Moisture Control技术亮点：低水分控制

Our specialized low moisture carbon black masterbatch for agricultural PE pipes guarantees a moisture content of ≤0.15%, eliminating surface blistering and line breakage during high-speed extrusion. This critical specification ensures uninterrupted production runs, even in high-humidity environments. 我们专业的农用PE管用低水分炭黑母粒确保水分含量≤0.15%，消除高速挤出过程中的表面起泡和生产线断裂。这项关键规格确保即使在高湿度环境中也能不间断生产。

Tech Highlight: Sub-micron Dispersion技术亮点：亚微米分散

How to improve carbon black masterbatch dispersion in PE pipe extrusion? Our technical team utilizes high-torque twin-screw compounding lines with precision screw geometry to achieve uniform sub-micron carbon black particle distribution, ensuring absolute anti-aging, UV shielding, and physical strength for long-life outdoor pipelines. 如何改善PE管挤出中炭黑母粒的分散性？我们的技术团队采用具有精密螺杆几何结构的高扭矩双螺杆复合生产线，实现均匀的亚微米炭黑颗粒分布，确保长寿命户外管道的绝对抗老化、紫外线防护和物理强度。
