Masterbatch & Plastic Coloring: Top 10 FAQs Decoded by a 15-Year Expert色母粒与塑料着色：15年专家解读10大常见问题

May 17, 2026

When sourcing masterbatch globally, procurement managers and plastics engineers often face critical challenges that impact both product quality and production costs. Based on the most-discussed topics on Quora regarding masterbatch and plastic modification, we have compiled and answered the top 10 questions to help you optimize your manufacturing process. 在全球采购色母粒时，采购经理和塑料工程师经常会遇到影响产品质量与生产成本的关键问题。基于 Quora 上关于色母粒与塑料改性的高频讨论话题，我们整理并回答了最常见的 10 个问题，帮助你优化制造流程。

1) Carrier Compatibility & Formulation1）载体相容性与配方

Q1: Can I use a PE-based masterbatch for PP injection molding?Q1：PP 注塑可以用 PE 载体的色母吗？

While PE and PP are both polyolefins, using a PE carrier in PP can lower the structural integrity and heat resistance of the final PP part due to different melting points (about 130°C for PE vs. 165°C for PP). For optimal mechanical properties, always match the carrier resin with the base polymer (e.g., PP carrier for PP matrix). 虽然 PE 和 PP 同属聚烯烃，但由于熔点不同（PE 约 130°C，而 PP 约 165°C），在 PP 中使用 PE 载体可能会降低成品的结构强度与耐热性。为了获得最佳力学性能，建议载体树脂与基材树脂匹配（例如 PP 基材选 PP 载体）。

Q2: What is the ideal let-in ratio (dosage) for color masterbatch?Q2：彩色色母的理想添加比例是多少？

Typically, the standard addition rate is 1% to 4%. For high-opacity applications like blown film, 3%–4% is recommended. For thick-wall injection molding, 1%–2% is sufficient. Exceeding 5% may cause processing issues and increase costs without improving color intensity. 一般标准添加量为 1%–4%。对于需要高遮盖的应用（如吹膜），建议 3%–4%；对于厚壁注塑，1%–2% 通常足够。超过 5% 往往不会显著提升着色强度，反而可能引发加工问题并增加成本。

Q3: How does the carrier resin affect the Melt Flow Rate (MFR)?Q3：载体树脂会如何影响熔融指数（MFR）？

The carrier resin should ideally have a higher MFR than the base polymer matrix. This ensures the masterbatch melts earlier during extrusion, allowing the pigments to distribute evenly throughout the polymer melt. 理想情况下，载体树脂的 MFR 应高于基材树脂，这样色母在挤出过程中更早熔融，有利于颜料在聚合物熔体中更快、更均匀地分散。

2) Pigment Performance & Quality Control2）颜料表现与质量控制

Q4: Why does color migration occur, and how do we prevent it?Q4：为什么会出现迁移（渗色/吐色），如何预防？

Migration happens when organic pigments dissolve into the plastic matrix (especially in flexible PVC or LDPE) and bleed to the surface. To prevent this, switch to inorganic pigments or high-molecular-weight organic pigments with excellent migration resistance (Rating 4–5). 当有机颜料在塑料基体中发生一定程度溶解（柔性 PVC、LDPE 等体系更常见）并向表面渗出时，就会出现迁移。解决方法包括：改用无机颜料，或选用高分子量、迁移等级更高（4–5 级）的有机颜料体系。

Q5: How can we strictly control color consistency (ΔE) across different batches?Q5：如何严格控制不同批次的色差（ΔE）？

Reliable manufacturers utilize twin-screw extruders with precise gravimetric feeding systems and perform spectrophotometer testing. At SLMMB, we guarantee a color tolerance of ΔE < 0.8 to ensure absolute consistency. 可靠的制造商会使用双螺杆挤出机配合高精度失重（称重）喂料系统，并通过分光测色仪进行检测。在 SLMMB，我们可保证色差容差 ΔE < 0.8，以确保批次一致性。

3) Sustainability & Global Compliance3）可持续与全球合规

Q6: What are the main challenges when coloring PCR (Post-Consumer Recycled) plastics?Q6：给 PCR（消费后再生料）着色的主要挑战是什么？

PCR resins usually have an inherent grayish or yellowish undertone and inconsistent MFR. To tackle this, we formulate custom PCR masterbatches integrated with optical brighteners and neutralizing pigments to achieve vibrant colors even on dark recycled bases. PCR 树脂通常带有偏灰或偏黄底色，且 MFR 波动更大。应对思路是做定制化 PCR 色母/改性母粒，将荧光增白剂与中和颜料纳入体系，即使在偏深的再生底材上也能得到更鲜艳的颜色表现。

Q7: How do you ensure masterbatch compliance for food-contact packaging?Q7：如何确保食品接触包装用色母的合规性？

All raw materials (pigments, carriers, additives) must be strictly audited against international regulations. Ensure your supplier provides certified compliance documentation for FDA, EU Regulation No 10/2011, and GB 4806. 需要对所有原材料（颜料、载体、助剂）按国际法规进行严格审核，并要求供应商提供可追溯的合规文件（如 FDA、欧盟 10/2011，以及 GB 4806 相关要求）。

4) Processing & Advanced Functionalization4）加工与功能复配

Q8: What pigment parameters are critical for high-speed film blowing?Q8：高速吹膜对颜料/色母有哪些关键指标要求？

You must specify Filter Pressure Value (FPV) and moisture content. For thin film applications (< 20 μm), the masterbatch should have FPV < 1.0 bar/g and moisture content < 0.15% to avoid pinholes and film breakage. 需要明确 FPV（过滤压差值）与含水率。对于薄膜应用（< 20 μm），建议色母满足 FPV < 1.0 bar/g、含水率 < 0.15%，以减少针孔与断膜风险。

Q9: Can color masterbatch be combined with functional properties?Q9：彩色色母能否与功能助剂复配成“二合一”？

Yes. Combo Masterbatches (2-in-1) that merge color with functional additives (such as anti-UV, anti-static, or antimicrobial properties) simplify inventory management and reduce production downtime for downstream converters. 可以。将颜色与功能助剂（如抗 UV、抗静电、抗菌等）组合成 2-in-1 复配母粒，可简化下游库存管理，减少换料/调机导致的停机时间。

Q10: How does Carbon Black particle size affect Black Masterbatch performance?Q10：炭黑粒径会如何影响黑色母粒性能？

Smaller particle sizes (15–25 nm) provide superior jetness and UV protection but are harder to disperse. Larger particle sizes (40–50 nm) offer better processability and cost efficiency but lower tinting strength. 更小的粒径（15–25 nm）通常带来更高黑度与更强 UV 防护，但分散难度更大；更大的粒径（40–50 nm）加工更友好、性价比更高，但着色力与黑度相对降低。
